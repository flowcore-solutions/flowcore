# FlowCore Phase 3 Topical Authority Execution

This plan converts the existing FlowCore SEO architecture into a real industrial authority system. It is designed for deep engineering content, Karnataka-local commercial intent, semantic internal linking, and E-E-A-T. It intentionally avoids shallow doorway pages and generic AI blog production.

## Strategic Positioning

FlowCore should rank and convert as a Karnataka industrial pump systems specialist, not as a generic pump catalogue. Every important page should answer three buyer questions:

- Will this pump work for my duty condition?
- Can FlowCore help select, supply, install, and service it locally?
- Does this page prove engineering judgment, not just keyword coverage?

Primary authority entities:

- FlowCore Solutions
- Berlington pumps
- industrial pumps
- vertical multistage pumps
- high pressure RO pumps
- boiler feed pumps
- sewage and STP pumps
- pressure booster systems
- fire fighting pump systems
- HVAC circulation pumps
- Bangalore, Mysore, Mangalore, Karnataka industrial corridors
- hospitals, hotels, manufacturing, pharmaceutical, commercial buildings, water treatment contractors

## Internal Linking Blueprint

Every page should link in four directions:

- Up to commercial hub
- Across to sibling technical content
- Down to application/industry/local pages
- Forward to service/contact intent

Contextual anchor patterns:

- Product to blog: "how vertical multistage pumps work", "common booster pump problems", "RO pump maintenance checklist"
- Blog to product: "vertical multistage pumps for RO and pressure boosting", "Berlington HYDRO booster systems", "NISO end-suction pumps for fire and HVAC duty"
- Blog to application: "RO plant pump selection", "pressure boosting systems", "HVAC circulation duty"
- Product to local: "vertical multistage pumps in Bangalore", "boiler feed pump support in Karnataka"
- Industry to product: "booster pumps for hospital pressure stability", "HVAC pumps for chilled water circulation"
- Service to product: "pump maintenance for vertical multistage and booster systems"

Placement rules:

- First third of page: one commercial hub link
- Middle technical section: two contextual technical links
- Application/industry section: three relevance links
- FAQ answers: one natural link only when it helps the answer
- CTA section: service/contact link
- Related resources block: product, blog, application, industry, city links

Avoid:

- exact-match anchor repetition across every city page
- footer-only SEO links
- unrelated product links inserted because a keyword matches
- city pages linking only to other city pages

## Content Quality Rules

Every serious page must include:

- a direct answer in the opening
- at least one table or checklist where useful
- engineering constraints and failure modes
- local Karnataka relevance
- related product/application/industry links
- a practical quote CTA
- FAQs written as real buyer questions
- "Last updated" freshness signal
- author/reviewer attribution where possible

Do not publish:

- city pages where only the city name changes
- 700-word generic product pages
- blogs without a commercial or semantic cluster role
- comparison pages that pretend one product type is always best
- service pages without process, scope, and local response detail
